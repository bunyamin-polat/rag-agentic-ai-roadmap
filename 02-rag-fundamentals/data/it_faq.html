IT Help Desk FAQ

Sample data for the roadmap notebooks. Brightleaf is a made-up company.

How do I reset my password?

Open the self-service portal and choose "Forgot password". You will get a code on your work phone. If you are locked out after five wrong tries, call the help desk on extension 4400.

How do I connect to the VPN?

Install the VPN app from the company software center and sign in with your work account. The VPN is required for the HR portal and the finance tools when you work outside the office. It is not needed for email or chat.

What is the guest Wi-Fi password?

Guests use the network "Brightleaf-Guest". The password changes every Monday and is shown on the screen at the reception desk. Employees should always use the network "Brightleaf-Staff" with their work account.

How do I get new software?

Most tools are available in the software center and install without approval. For any other software, open a ticket with the name of the tool and the reason you need it. Paid licenses need your team lead's approval and usually take three working days.

My laptop is slow or broken. What now?

Restart it first and install all updates. If the problem stays, open a ticket. The help desk can give you a loan laptop on the same day while yours is repaired. Laptops are replaced every four years.

How do I print?

There is one printer on every floor. Send your document to "Brightleaf-Print" and release it at any printer with your badge. Color printing is limited to 50 pages per month.

Are my files backed up?

Files in the company cloud drive are backed up every night and can be restored for 90 days. Files saved only on your laptop are not backed up, so keep your work in the cloud drive.

How do I report a phishing email?

Do not click any links. Use the "Report phishing" button in the mail app, then delete the email. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, call the help desk right away and change your password.
