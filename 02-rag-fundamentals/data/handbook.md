# Brightleaf Employee Handbook

Brightleaf is a made-up software company. This handbook is sample data for the roadmap
notebooks. Every name, number, and rule in it is invented.

Version 3.2, valid from January 2026.

## 1. Working hours

Our core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Friday. Inside core hours, everyone should be
reachable for meetings. Outside core hours, you can plan your day freely, as long as you work
40 hours per week on average.

Overtime must be approved by your team lead before you work it. Approved overtime is paid back
as free time within three months. Brightleaf does not pay overtime in cash.

## 2. Remote work

You can work from home up to three days per week. Every team agrees on one shared office day,
so that people meet in person at least once a week.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Tell
People Operations at least two weeks before you travel, because of tax and insurance rules.

Brightleaf pays a home office allowance of 30 EUR per month for internet and electricity. New
employees also receive a one-time budget of 400 EUR for a desk chair and a monitor.

## 3. Vacation

Full-time employees get 28 vacation days per calendar year. Part-time employees get vacation
days in proportion to their working days.

Request vacation in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for trips of three days or
more. Shorter breaks can be requested one day before.

Up to five unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be
used by 31 March, otherwise they expire.

## 4. Sick leave

If you are sick, tell your team lead before 10:00 on the first day. From the fourth day of
sickness, a doctor's note is required. Upload it in the HR portal.

You do not need to make up for sick days, and they never reduce your vacation days.

## 5. Parental leave

Parents can take up to 16 weeks of paid parental leave in the first two years after a child is
born or adopted. The leave can be split into at most four blocks.

After parental leave, you can work part-time (between 20 and 32 hours per week) for up to one
year and then return to full-time.

## 6. Expenses and travel

Book business trips through the travel tool. Train travel is preferred for trips shorter than
six hours. Flights must be in economy class.

The daily meal allowance on business trips is 45 EUR within the country and 60 EUR abroad.
Hotels may cost up to 150 EUR per night, or 200 EUR in capital cities.

Submit receipts in the expense app within 30 days. Expenses above 500 EUR need approval from
your department head before you spend the money.

## 7. Company vehicles

Brightleaf has four shared electric cars for client visits. Book them in the car-sharing app.

Eating and drinking in company vehicles is not allowed, except for water. Smoking and vaping
are never allowed. Leave the car clean and charge it to at least 50% before you return it.

Private trips with company vehicles are not allowed. Parking fines and speeding tickets are
paid by the driver.

## 8. Equipment and IT security

Every employee receives a laptop and a phone. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the
IT help desk within 24 hours.

Use the password manager for all work accounts and turn on two-factor authentication.
Passwords must have at least 14 characters.

Do not install software from unknown sources. Company data may only be stored in approved
cloud services, never on private USB sticks.

## 9. Mobile phone policy

The company phone is for work. Short private calls are fine, but private apps that collect
location data are not allowed on the work phone.

Phone costs are covered up to 40 EUR per month. Roaming must be switched off on private trips.

Do not use your phone while driving a company vehicle, not even with a hands-free kit.

## 10. Learning and growth

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 EUR per year for courses, books, and conferences.
In addition, you get five paid learning days per year.

Unused learning budget cannot be carried over to the next year. If you leave Brightleaf within
six months after a course that cost more than 800 EUR, you must pay back half of the cost.

## 11. Code of conduct

Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are not tolerated in any
form, online or offline.

Gifts from clients or suppliers worth more than 50 EUR must be declared to People Operations.
Report problems to your team lead, to People Operations, or anonymously through the speak-up
form.

## 12. Health and safety

The office has first-aid kits in the kitchen and next to the main entrance. Every floor has at
least two trained first-aid helpers; their names are on the notice board.

In case of fire, use the stairs, never the elevator, and meet at the parking lot across the
street. A fire drill takes place twice a year.

## 13. Contact

People Operations: questions about contracts, leave, and benefits.
IT help desk: laptops, phones, accounts, and security incidents.
Facilities: office access, desks, and the company cars.
